PROJECT / PUBLISHED · IEEE COMMUNICATIONS LETTERS 2025

Semantic Communication-Aware Federated Fine-Tuning of Large Language Models

How can language models be fine-tuned across devices without repeatedly transmitting full intermediate representations?

Samia Rahman & Ratun RahmanPeer-reviewed journal article · 2025LLM fine-tuning / Semantic communication / Federated learning
Read publisher article ↗
>90%Reported reduction in communication volume
≤1%Accuracy gap from centralized baselines, as reported

Motivation

Federated fine-tuning faces two resource bottlenecks: exchanging intermediate activations and gradients, and assigning model partitions to clients with different compute and communication resources. A naive setup can make adaptation expensive or slow.

Core idea

01Semantic autograd bridge

Compress intermediate tensor communication into low-dimensional semantic representations across forward and backward passes.

02Compression-aware participation

Choose clients and partition allocation with resource constraints in mind.

03End-to-end fine-tuning

Preserve language-model task performance while reducing communication demand.

Reported evaluation

The peer-reviewed article evaluates WikiText-2 and AG News, reporting over 90% lower communication volume with task accuracy within approximately 1% of centralized baselines. These are results reported in the paper, not independently reproduced benchmarks on this portfolio.
